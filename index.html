Bulk timetable converter

This is a simple tool to help quickly publish a schedule on Discord in the viewer's own timezone

Discord supports a markup syntax like <t:1785619373:F> that makes it possible to post the date and time of an event that has attendees in multiple timezones (and many ways of writing down times and dates) so that no one needs do timezone math in their head, keep track of which other parts of the world switch to on Daylight Saving Time when, or get confused about whether 8/1 means August 1 (US) or January 8 (UK). Great for Zoom sessions with farflung attendees!

There are several tools such as HammerTime and DiscordTimestamp for converting a single date and time into the right markup for displaying a date and/or time or a countdown. (The FAQs on those sites may answer some of the questions you may have.)

But what if you have an all-day event and want to announce not just the start time but a whole a schedule? It's tedious to convert the times one at a time. So this tool will bulk-convert a whole day's times for you. (For a three-day event, of course, just use it three times.)

This tool's inputs are not internationalized, but don't worry, Discord's output is.
  • You'll be inputting the times in a single timezone. You can change it in the pulldown menu. The default is your browser's local timezone (unless the URL specifies ?defaultTZ=). But the computer-readable output is generic, and Discord will be in different timezones for different attendees.
  • Similarly, this tool uses English month names for input and preview purposes, but Discord will display the pasted output in the Discord user's language.
  • For simplicity for users in countries like the U.S. that use 12-hour times, this tool accepts 12-hour format with or without an explicit am and pm and tries to disambiguate. It also accepts 24-hour time.
