Bulk timetable converter

This is a simple tool to help quickly publish a schedule on Discord in the viewer's own timezone

Discord supports a markup syntax like <t:1785619373:F> that makes it possible to post the date and time of an event that has attendees in multiple timezones (and ways of writing down times and dates, like month/day/year in the U.S. vs day/month/year in the U.K. and day.month.year in some other countries) so that no one needs to convert on their own or get confused about whether 8/1 means August 1 or January 8. Great for Zoom sessions with farflung attendees! There are several tools such as HammerTime and DiscordTimestamp for converting a single date and time into the right markup for displaying a date and/or time or a countdown. (The FAQs on those sites may answer some of the questions you may have.)

The number is "just" the number of seconds since the number of non-leap seconds that have elapsed since 00:00:00 UTC on 1 January 1970, aka "Unix time". Obviously you can't do that conversion in your head, but so you need a tool to do it, and there are several.

But what if you have an all-day event and want to announce not just the start time but a whole a schedule? It's tedious to convert the times one at a time. So this tool will bulk-convert a whole day's times for you. (For a three-day event, of course, just use it three times.)

This tool's inputs are not internationalized, but don't worry, Discord is.
  • You'll be inputting the times in a single timezone, of course, as with other such tools, whichever timezone you specify. But the output displayed on Discord will be in different timezones for different attendees.
  • Similarly, this tool uses English month names for input and preview purposes, but Discord will display the pasted output in the Discord user's language.
  • For simplicity for U.S. users, this tool accepts 12-hour format with or without an explicit am and pm and tries to disambiguate. It also accepts 24-hour time.
All of this is just the way you get the correct information input. The output will be identical and Discord will display it for the proper locale.
